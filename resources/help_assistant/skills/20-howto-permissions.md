Explain the model in plain words first: people belong to groups; groups get data permissions (what data they can query) and collection permissions (what saved content they can see or edit). Then give the steps for their specific goal. Mention that the "All Users" group is the baseline everyone gets, so restricting access usually starts by tightening "All Users".
